How to play

Take a job from the Orders tab, then tap the machine on the Floor tab as the orange marker crosses the green band. A well-timed tap does four times the work of a mistimed one and raises the part's quality, which is what it pays. Jobs move themselves to the next machine they need, and ship when the last operation finishes.

The ? button beside "Shop Floor" opens the in-game manual: every gain and loss in the game, spelled out - what reputation is worth, what a late job costs, and what your operators do while the app is closed.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress stored? On the device, in the app itself. There are no accounts and no servers, so progress does not follow you to another phone.
  • I lost my shop. Clearing the app's data, or the browser's site data on the web version, erases the save. There is no backup to restore from.
  • Does it need the internet? No. Once installed it runs offline.
  • Are there ads? No, and there never have been.
  • What can I buy? The iPhone and Android apps sell optional extras at the bottom of the Upgrades tab: the Union Contract (every job pays double, for good), the Night Crew (operators work twice as hard while you are away) and the Big Contract (a one-off cash boost). The whole game is playable without them.
  • I bought something and it is missing. Tap Restore purchases under the store, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with. Resetting the shop or selling up never removes a purchase.
  • How do I start over? Reset shop, at the bottom of the Upgrades tab.

Reporting a problem

It helps to include the device and OS version, the version number shown at the bottom of the Upgrades tab, and what you were doing when it happened.
